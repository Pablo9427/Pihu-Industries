Corporate Profile

Pioneering Precision Manufacturing

Engineering Excellence at Satharia Industrial Area

PIHU INDUSTRIES is an emerging manufacturing company based in Satharia, Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, specializing in high-performance Stainless Steel Wire Mesh, SS Jali, and customized filtration media.

Designed to serve critical industries requiring high tensile strength, chemical stability, and exact aperture sizes for filtration, screening, separation, and sieving.

Workflow

Manufacturing Process

01

Wire Selection

Selection of chemical-certified stainless steel wire (SS 304, 304L, 316, 316L) according to application requirements.

02

Wire Preparation

Winding and tension control using automatic wire feeding systems to prevent tension variation.

03

Precision Weaving

Interlacing of warp and weft wires on heavy looms to form accurate aperture openings.

04

Quality Inspection

Microscopic verification of opening uniformity, wire gauge accuracy, and alignment stability.

05

Cutting & Finishing

Sizing into complete rolls, flat sheets, or die-stamped circular filter discs.

06

Safe Packaging

Moisture-proof wrapping and protective casing to guarantee intact delivery.
